## Rue's answers in explore (2026-10-15)

- Vote corrections: during the sweep only the n-th under the hand counts; in the confirm step after the sweep votes can be toggled by tap before the result is final.
- Deck voting slide: same new look as the game (constant hand, red n-th, dynamic ticks).
- Marking players: while a show-bar text is open (in the circle centre), taps mark players (several, up to all); closing the text clears the marks, then taps select targets as before.
- Text cursor / text selection: off on UI text across the whole app; input fields keep their caret.
- "Die Uhr" is the day countdown pill: top right of the day screen's head, below the app header, scrolls with the content.
- Bluffs: the text under the show-bar goes; "Neu würfeln" stays (blind re-draw).
